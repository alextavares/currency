## What Is a Forex Heatmap?

A forex heatmap is a **visual grid** that displays the performance of every major currency pair using colors. Green means the pair is gaining (bullish), red means it's losing (bearish), and the color intensity shows how strong the move is.

Think of it as a weather radar for the forex market. Instead of scanning 28 different charts, you get a **single screen** that instantly shows you where the action is.

The [LiveForexStrength heatmap](/heatmap) covers all 28 pairs formed by the 8 major currencies and updates in real time.

## How to Read a Forex Heatmap

### The Color System

| Color | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 🟢 Dark Green | Strong bullish move (+0.5% or more) | High momentum — look for buy setups |
| 🟢 Light Green | Mild bullish move (+0.1% to +0.5%) | Slight positive bias |
| ⬜ Gray/White | Flat, little movement | No opportunity — avoid |
| 🔴 Light Red | Mild bearish move (-0.1% to -0.5%) | Slight negative bias |
| 🔴 Dark Red | Strong bearish move (-0.5% or more) | High momentum — look for sell setups |

### Reading the Grid

The heatmap grid has currencies on two axes:
- **Rows** = Base currency (the currency you buy)
- **Columns** = Quote currency (the currency you sell)

When the cell for AUD/JPY is **dark green**, it means AUD is strengthening against JPY. When it's **dark red**, AUD is weakening against JPY.

**Pro tip**: Look for **rows that are mostly green** — that currency is strong across the board. Look for **rows that are mostly red** — that currency is weak everywhere.

## 5 Trading Strategies Using the Heatmap

### Strategy 1: The "Dark Green Row" Trade

When you see an entire **row glowing dark green**, that base currency is strong against everything. This is a high-confidence long signal.

**How to trade it:**
1. Find a row that is mostly dark green
2. Within that row, find the **darkest cell** — that's the pair with the most momentum
3. Open that pair's chart and enter long

### Strategy 2: The "Dark Red Row" Trade

The opposite of Strategy 1. When an entire **row is dark red**, that base currency is weak against everything.

**How to trade it:**
1. Find the reddest row
2. Identify the darkest red cell — that pair is falling the hardest
3. Open that pair and look for short/sell entries

### Strategy 3: The Divergence Spotter

Sometimes the heatmap reveals that a currency is strong against most pairs but weak against one. This **divergence** can signal a reversal in the outlier pair.

**Example**: GBP row is green for every column except CHF (which is red). This suggests GBP/CHF might be due for a reversal back to green as it "catches up" with GBP's overall strength.

### Strategy 4: Session Open Momentum

At major session opens (London at 08:00 UTC, New York at 13:00 UTC), the heatmap often shifts dramatically. Watch the heatmap **in the first 30 minutes** of a session to spot emerging trends.

**How to use it:**
1. Check the heatmap at 08:00 UTC (London open)
2. Note which currencies flip from gray/flat to green or red
3. These session-opening moves often continue for 2-4 hours

### Strategy 5: Cross-Pair Discovery

Most traders only watch USD pairs. The heatmap shows you cross-pairs (like EUR/AUD, GBP/NZD, CAD/CHF) that might have **stronger moves** than the popular pairs.

**Example**: EUR/USD might be flat (gray), but EUR/AUD could be dark green — the EUR is strong, but it's showing against AUD, not against USD.

## How the Heatmap Calculates Values

The [LiveForexStrength heatmap](/heatmap) calculates the **percentage change** of each pair over the selected timeframe. The process:

1. **Capture the opening price** at the start of the timeframe
2. **Compare with the current price** continuously
3. **Calculate percentage change** = (Current - Open) / Open × 100
4. **Map to color** based on the magnitude of the change

This gives you an objective, mathematical view of the market — no opinions, no bias, just data.

## Common Heatmap Patterns

### Pattern 1: "The Wall"
When one currency shows **all green** while another shows **all red**, you have a clear directional bias. This is the most profitable pattern — pair them together.

### Pattern 2: "The Checkered Board"
When colors alternate randomly with no clear pattern, the market is **choppy and directionless**. Best to stay out and wait for clarity.

### Pattern 3: "The Split"
Risk-on/risk-off scenario: AUD, NZD, CAD show green while JPY, CHF show red (or vice versa). This tells you the market is trading on risk sentiment. Trade accordingly.

### Pattern 4: "The Fade"
A currency that was dark green an hour ago is now light green or turning gray. The momentum is fading. Consider taking profits on existing positions.
